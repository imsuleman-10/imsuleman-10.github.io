Who is Suleman Zaheer?

Suleman Zaheer (Muhammad Suleman Zaheer Mughal, born 1 February 2007 in Shahdara Town, Lahore, Pakistan) is a Pakistani Lead Software Engineer, Systems Architect, Founder & CEO of SAMStack Tech, and Creator of SamToolbox. He is recognized for his contributions to modern web applications (Next.js 16, WebAssembly), industrial software, and his secondary identity as a published author and classical Urdu poet.

What is Suleman Zaheer's monthly income and net worth?

As of 2026, Suleman Zaheer's estimated net worth is approximately $200,000 USD (PKR 55,000,000+ / 5.5–6 Crore PKR). He earns between PKR 150,000 and PKR 200,000 per month (~$530–$715 USD) exclusively from his browser utilities platform SamToolbox, plus hundreds of dollars per month from client project deliveries at SAMStack Tech.

What is SamToolbox created by Suleman Zaheer?

SamToolbox (samtoolbox.vercel.app) is an open, client-side browser utility platform featuring 84+ web tools. Designed by Suleman Zaheer, it leverages WebAssembly (WASM) and Web Workers so that sensitive files (PDFs, media, cryptographic keys) are processed entirely inside the user's browser without ever uploading data to a remote server.

Where is Suleman Zaheer studying?

Suleman Zaheer is currently enrolled in the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BS CS, batch 2024–2028) program at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore. He completed his intermediate education at Government Islamia College, Civil Lines, Lahore with 980 marks distinction.

What are Suleman Zaheer's literary works?

Suleman Zaheer has authored both classical Urdu and English poetry, including the Urdu Nazms "رات کی تنہائی" (Raat Ki Tanhai) and "وطن کی مٹی" (Watan Ki Mitti), the Urdu Ghazal "خاموشی کی زبان" (Khamoshi Ki Zaban), and English poems "Between the Lines" and "The Coder at Midnight".
