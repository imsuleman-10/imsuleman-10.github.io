Executive Biography

Muhammad Suleman Zaheer Mughal (professionally known as Suleman Zaheer; Urdu: سلیمان ظہیر) was born on 1 February 2007 in Shahdara Town, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan into a respected Mughal family. His father, Muhammad Zaheer, is a prominent businessman in Lahore, and his brother, Abdullah Zaheer, is a professional Mechanical Engineer and UET graduate.

Suleman serves as the Founder & CEO of SAMStack Studio, a software consultancy delivering modern web applications, distributed ERP systems, Flutter mobile platforms, and AI integrations. He is the creator of SamToolbox, a suite of 84+ WebAssembly browser utilities, and the author of the published open-source NPM package @imsuleman-10/samtoolbox-core.

Academic Trajectory

Zaheer completed his Intermediate in Computer Science (ICS) with academic distinction (980 marks) at Government Islamia College, Civil Lines, Lahore. In 2024, he entered the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS, Batch 2024–2028).
