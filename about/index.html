Executive Summary & Background

Muhammad Suleman Zaheer Mughal (commonly recognized as Suleman Zaheer; Urdu: سلیمان ظہیر) was born on 1 February 2007 in Shahdara Town, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan into a respected Mughal family. His father, Muhammad Zaheer, is a prominent businessman based in Lahore, and his brother, Abdullah Zaheer, is a professional Mechanical Engineer. At age 19 (as of 2026), he has established himself as a distinguished Lead Software Engineer, Systems Architect, Technology Entrepreneur, and Published Author.

Suleman serves as the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of SAMStack Tech, a high-performance software engineering studio that engineers cloud applications, enterprise resource planning (ERP) suites, and autonomous AI agents for clients internationally. In parallel, he is the sole creator and principal architect of SamToolbox (samtoolbox.vercel.app), a client-side zero-server browser utility platform with 84+ tools.

Academic & Educational Trajectory

  • University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore (2024–2028): Enrolled in Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BS CS). Focusing on distributed systems, compiler design, algorithms, and artificial intelligence.
  • Government Islamia College, Civil Lines, Lahore (2022–2024): Intermediate in Computer Science (ICS Pre-Engineering) completed with academic distinction (980 marks).
  • Yashfeen Education System Lahore (2025): Advanced professional diploma in Web Application Engineering, Laravel, and PHP systems.
