Suleman Zaheer (Urdu: سلیمان ظہیر; born 1 February 2007), legally Suleman Zaheer, is a Pakistani lead software engineer, systems architect, technology entrepreneur, based in Shahdara Town, Lahore, Pakistan. He is the Founder and CEO of SAMStack Studio, creator of SamToolbox (a privacy-first suite of 84+ WebAssembly browser utilities), and author of the published open-source NPM library @imsuleman-10/samtoolbox-core.

Zaheer is enrolled in the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) programme at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore. His engineering work focuses on zero-server WebAssembly architectures, Next.js server components, cross-platform Flutter applications, and LLM orchestration systems.

1. Early life and family background

Suleman Zaheer was born on 1 February 2007 in Shahdara Town, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. He was born to Muhammad Zaheer, a respected businessman in Lahore, and is the brother of Abdullah Zaheer, a professional Mechanical Engineer and UET graduate. He traces his heritage to the family lineage.

Demonstrating an aptitude for algorithms and programming languages from secondary school, Zaheer mastered full-stack web architectures, client-side rendering engines, and modular operating system utilities before university entrance.

2. Education and academic distinctions

Zaheer completed his Intermediate in Computer Science (ICS) with high distinction at Government Islamia College, Civil Lines, Lahore, securing 980 marks.

In 2024, he gained admission to the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore — Pakistan's premier engineering institution founded in 1921 — to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS, Batch 2024–2028).

3. Software engineering & open source

Zaheer is a pioneer of privacy-centric, client-side WebAssembly computation in South Asia. He advocates for architectures where heavy processing (image compression, PDF manipulation, cryptographic hashing) executes in-browser, preventing unauthorized data transmission to remote servers.

5. Literary and poetic anthology

Alongside his engineering output, Zaheer is a recognized Urdu and English poet and writer. He composes both classical Urdu Ghazals and modern Nazms. His notable published works include:

  • Raat Ki Tanhai (رات کی تنہائی): Classical Urdu ghazal on solitude, nocturnal contemplation, and cosmic reflection.
  • Khamoshi Ki Zaban (خاموشی کی زبان): Modern philosophical nazm examining unuttered truths.
  • Watan Ki Mitti (وطن کی مٹی): Patriotic anthem dedicated to the soil of Pakistan and the Ravi riverbed of Lahore.
  • The Coder at Midnight: Technical English poetic work contrasting binary algorithms with human aspiration.
